// THE DETECTIVE'S METHOD

How we catch a trend

01

Find the variable

Pull variables measured by NASA missions or produced by NASA models — sea surface height, ice mass, CO₂, temperature anomalies, fire radiative power.

02

Watch it over time

Visualize decades of measurements as time series so any consistent direction — up, down, thinning, intensifying — becomes visible.

03

Test for significance

A trend is only real if it's significant. We check confidence, magnitude, and whether the same process pushes different regions in opposite directions.

04

Tell the story

Pin every confirmed trend to the exact place on Earth where it happens — this page is the detective's evidence board.

// ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge

This landing page is the public face of our hackathon project. It uses NASA open data themes — sea level rise, land & sea ice loss, CO₂ growth, global temperature, wildfire activity, and groundwater decline — rendered as an interactive 3D Earth you can spin and investigate.

Data themes are illustrative of NASA Earth observations (GRACE-FO, Jason-3, Sentinel-6, OCO-2, MODIS, NOAA global temperature records).

// THE DETECTIVE'S METHOD

How we catch a trend

01

Select the Variable

Choose from variables measured by NASA missions or produced by Earth science models: CO2, sea level, ice mass, temperature, wildfire risk or groundwater.

02

Fetch the Data

Live data is pulled from NOAA GML and Open-Meteo ERA5 APIs where available. When APIs are offline, curated real-measurement archives are served instead, always clearly labelled.

03

Test for Significance

The Mann-Kendall test, the non-parametric method Earth scientists use, computes S, Z, a p-value and Sen's slope. A trend is reported significant only when p < 0.05.

04

Pin It to Earth

Every confirmed trend is anchored to the exact place where it is measured. Click anywhere on the globe to surface the regional trend at that coordinate.

// ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge

Developed by Ritish & Sun Jin · The Data Detectives

TerraTrend is an interactive Earth science tool that selects variables from NASA missions, visualises how they change over time, runs the Mann-Kendall statistical significance test and pins every confirmed trend to the exact location on Earth where it is measured.

Live data: NOAA GML Mauna Loa · Open-Meteo ERA5. Archival data: GRACE-FO, Jason-3 / Sentinel-6, GISTEMP v4.
